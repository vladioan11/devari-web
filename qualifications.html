Surveyor Qualifications & Technical Accreditations

Devari’s inspection personnel hold industry-standard accreditations covering professional marine surveying, cryogenic LNG cargo containment systems, and advanced Level III protective coating verification.

ACCREDITATION 01

FROSIO Level III Inspector

Certified Surface Treatment Inspector — Peer Review & Master Quality Auditor Grade.

Surface Treatment Inspector (Level III)

Certified in accordance with NS 476. Authorized for independent third-party audits, resolving specification disputes, ambient monitoring, and compliance verification for marine structures and IMO PSPC ballast tanks.

Regulatory Scope

Substrate preparation assessment (ISO 8501-1), climatic dew-point monitoring (ISO 8502-4), pull-off adhesion testing (ISO 4624), and Dry Film Thickness (DFT) surveys (ISO 19840 / SSPC-PA2).

ACCREDITATION 02

GTT Mark III Cargo Containment

Gaztransport & Technigaz (GTT) Specialized Cryogenic Membrane Systems.

Mark III Membrane System Training

Specialized qualification covering the architecture, installation oversight, primary corrugated stainless steel barrier verification, and secondary triplex membrane integrity for LNG carriers and bunkering vessels.

Operational Scope

Membrane joint fit-up inspection, tightness testing (ammonia / vacuum box testing), plywood insulation box alignment, and mastics application quality assurance under cryogenic standards.

ACCREDITATION 03

Marine Surveying & Jotun Academy

Comprehensive Vessel Inspection & Specialized Protective Paint Advisory.

Diploma in Marine Surveying

Formal academic qualification governing hull structural surveys, cargo gear audits, outfitting inspection, drydock project monitoring, and IACS classification framework compliance.

Jotun Coating Advisory Academy

Technical certification in high-performance marine coatings, anti-fouling biocide mechanisms, hull efficiency optimization, epoxy ballast tank systems, and paint manufacturer application standards.
